Amirreza Hosseini

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Engineering Management at Stevens Institute of Technology.

My research brings together optimization, reinforcement learning, and systems engineering to support decision-making in complex engineering enterprises. I focus on resource-constrained project scheduling and the management of megaprojects.

I use hetero-functional graph theory and model-based systems engineering to connect how systems are designed with how their resources and activities are planned, scheduled, and operated.
